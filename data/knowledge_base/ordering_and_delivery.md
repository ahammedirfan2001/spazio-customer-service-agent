# Spazio Restaurant — Ordering, Payment and Delivery

## Ordering channels
Spazio accepts orders directly through phone calls, WhatsApp and walk-in customers. It currently does not accept orders through third-party food delivery applications.

Customers can order for dine-in, takeaway or home delivery.

## Order confirmation
An order is considered confirmed only after restaurant staff have checked availability and confirmed the order with the customer. An enquiry or message alone is not confirmation.

## Payment methods
Spazio currently accepts cash and UPI.

## Delivery operations
Spazio uses its own delivery staff. The two outlets have different delivery areas. Delivery is generally available within approximately **5 km** of each outlet, but may sometimes be possible beyond that range depending on the location and arrangements.

There is no fixed minimum order amount. Delivery charges may apply depending on factors such as the order bill amount or delivery distance. Customers should confirm the charge with the relevant outlet.

## Delivery time and order status
Delivery time depends on kitchen workload, food preparation requirements and delivery distance. There is no fixed delivery-time guarantee.

Customers can contact the outlet where they placed the order by phone or WhatsApp to ask for an update. Spazio does not currently provide automatic order-status notifications or live order tracking.

## Delivery problems
Customers should contact the relevant outlet promptly if an order is incorrect, damaged or unsatisfactory. Staff or the manager will review the issue. Depending on the situation, a replacement or refund may be considered. The assistant must not promise either outcome before staff confirmation.

## Assistant rules
- Never claim an order is confirmed until staff confirm it.
- Never promise free delivery or invent a delivery charge.
- Treat the approximate 5 km range as a guideline, not a strict boundary.
- Never invent a delivery time.
- Direct order-status enquiries to the outlet that handled the order.
