# Spazio Restaurant — Food Quality, Hygiene and Dietary Information

## Ingredient sourcing and checks
Spazio works with regular vendors. When ingredients are delivered, the restaurant checks their quality and freshness before accepting them for use.

## Kitchen hygiene
Spazio reports daily kitchen cleaning, regular cleaning of cooking equipment and utensils, separate storage arrangements for raw meat, vegetables and cooked food, and staff personal hygiene practices.

Spazio operates a central kitchen at Puthanangadi and separate kitchens at both outlets. Some food is prepared centrally and supplied to the outlets; other food is prepared at the outlet kitchens.

## Vegetarian and non-vegetarian preparation
Spazio serves both vegetarian and non-vegetarian food. Some kitchen facilities are shared.

The assistant must not claim that vegetarian food is prepared in a completely separate kitchen or guarantee that a dish is free from cross-contact. Customers with strict vegetarian requirements should contact the relevant outlet.

## Halal and meat sourcing
Spazio sources meat from halal suppliers but does **not** currently hold formal halal certification. The assistant must never describe Spazio as officially halal-certified.

## Allergies and allergens
Spazio does not currently maintain a complete allergen list. Customers with food allergies or dietary concerns must contact the relevant outlet and speak with staff or the kitchen before ordering.

Assistant safety rules:
- Never claim a dish is allergen-free.
- Never guess allergen content.
- Never guarantee that an ingredient can be removed.
- Direct allergy-related questions to the relevant outlet.
- Do not make claims about cross-contamination controls unless confirmed.

## Food customisation
Spazio accepts requests such as less spicy or extra spicy food and ingredient changes, where possible. Customisation depends on the dish, ingredient availability and kitchen requirements. Do not guarantee every request can be accommodated.

## Menu availability
Menu items are subject to availability. Availability can vary depending on ingredients, preparation requirements and outlet. The assistant must not assume an item is currently available just because it appears in the menu database. Customers should confirm with the relevant outlet.
