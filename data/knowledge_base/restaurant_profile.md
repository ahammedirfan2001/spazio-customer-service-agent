# Spazio Restaurant — Profile, History and Brand

## About Spazio

Spazio Restaurant began its journey in the food and hospitality industry around 1990. In 2018, the restaurant underwent a major renovation and expansion, introducing Chinese and Arabian cuisines and adopting the name "Spazio". The business continued to grow, opening its second outlet in Malaparamba in 2025.

Spazio has more than 35 years of experience in the food and hospitality industry.

## Brand philosophy
Spazio is built around three core values:

- **Quality:** Fresh ingredients, food preparation, kitchen hygiene and consistent taste.
- **Trust:** Spazio serves customers only food that the owners themselves would confidently eat.
- **Customer experience:** Spazio aims to provide good food at reasonable prices.

## Food quality standards
Spazio's stated practices include checking the quality and freshness of delivered ingredients, daily kitchen cleaning, regular cleaning of equipment and utensils, separate storage arrangements for raw meat, vegetables and cooked food, and staff personal hygiene practices.

## Customers and atmosphere
Spazio serves a broad range of customers, with students, working professionals and local residents forming its primary customer base. It also serves families and travellers.

The restaurant has a casual dining atmosphere and a family dining area. Baby chairs are available.

## What makes Spazio special
Spazio's identity is shaped by its roots dating back to around 1990, its owner-led food quality principle, its multi-cuisine menu, reasonable prices, two outlets, and services ranging from everyday dining to event catering.

## Future vision
Spazio aims to grow into a restaurant chain by establishing more company-owned outlets and eventually expanding into other cities and states, while maintaining quality, trust and customer experience.
