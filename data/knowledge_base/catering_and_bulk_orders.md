# Spazio Restaurant — Catering and Bulk Orders

## Catering services
Spazio provides catering at customers' event venues for weddings, engagements, birthday parties, family functions, corporate events, college events and other special occasions.

A catering arrangement can include food preparation and cooking, transportation of food to the venue, serving staff, buffet setup and serving counters, plates, cutlery and other dining arrangements.

Spazio can consider specific customer requirements. The exact arrangement depends on the event and what is agreed with the customer.

Customers should contact an outlet to discuss the event, guest count, venue, menu, service requirements and quotation.

## Bulk and advance food orders
Spazio accepts advance orders for large quantities of food, such as bulk biriyani orders for gatherings and events.

### Recommended notice
- Spazio prefers large orders to be placed two or more days in advance.
- At least one day's notice is generally preferred.
- Orders of up to 100 portions may be possible with at least 12 hours' notice.
- Some food items require longer preparation time. Required notice depends on the dishes, quantity and kitchen requirements.

The restaurant must confirm whether it can accept the order and meet the requested timing.

### Details to collect
Ask for the event/order type, food items and quantities, date and required time, event or delivery location, collection/delivery/full catering requirements, and special requests.

## Advance payment
An advance payment is required for large orders. The amount and arrangements must be confirmed with the relevant outlet. Do not invent a fixed amount or percentage.

## Cancellation and refunds
If the customer cancels before preparation begins, the advance payment may be refundable. If cancellation happens after preparation has started, the advance is generally not refundable because ingredients may have been purchased and preparation may be underway. The manager or owner makes the final decision on a specific case.

## Order modifications
Customers may request changes to a confirmed large order before preparation begins. Changes may include quantities, menu items or delivery/collection time. All changes depend on ingredient availability, kitchen capacity and confirmation by the outlet. Requests after preparation begins must be reviewed by the manager.

## Assistant rules
Do not invent catering prices, quotations or availability. Do not guarantee acceptance based only on the notice period. Do not confirm an order or change; staff must confirm it. Refer unusual requests and refund decisions to the manager or owner.
