## Kitchens
Spazio's central kitchen is located at Puthanangadi. Both outlets also have separate kitchens. Some food is prepared centrally and supplied to both outlets; other items are prepared at the respective outlet.

## Parking and accessibility
Both outlets have limited parking space.

Spazio does not have dedicated wheelchair-accessible facilities. If a customer has difficulty entering the restaurant, staff can serve food directly to their vehicle. Customers should contact the outlet before visiting so staff can make arrangements.

## Assistant guidance
Ask which outlet the customer means if unclear. Share only the matching outlet's contact details, Maps link and Instagram account. Never mix up outlet information.
